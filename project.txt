=========================================================
      FIELD TRACKING SYSTEM - PRODUCT OVERVIEW
=========================================================

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
---------------------------------------------------------
The Field Tracking System is a comprehensive, mobile-friendly enterprise solution designed to manage, monitor, and optimize the daily activities of on-field sales and marketing executives. The platform provides real-time visibility into workforce operations, automates attendance and travel allowances, and streamlines task management, HR operations, and payroll processing.

2. TARGET AUDIENCE & ROLES
---------------------------------------------------------
- Admin: Full system oversight and configuration.
- HR: Human Resources, leave tracking, & Payroll administration.
- Manager: Team oversight, task assignment, and approval workflows.
- Executive: On-field employees interacting with clients and performing daily tasks.

3. CORE SPECIFICATIONS & FEATURES
---------------------------------------------------------
A. Attendance & Geolocation Tracking
   - Geo-fenced check-in and check-out with automatic GPS coordinate logging.
   - Address resolution using Reverse Geocoding.
   - Mandatory photo verification (selfie) during check-ins/outs.
   - Odometer reading and photo capture for accurate travel distance tracking.
   - Periodic background location tracking for continuous field visibility.

B. Task & Client Meeting Management
   - Detailed task assignment workflows (Assigned by Manager -> Executed by Field Staff).
   - Bifurcation of tasks (e.g., Site Visits vs. In-House operations).
   - Client meeting logs including outcomes, notes, exact meeting locations, and selfie verification.
   - Multi-stage approval pipeline with robust audit trails.

C. Target & Performance Monitoring
   - Assign and track dynamic targets (Visits, Sales Amounts, New Clients, Promotions).
   - Daily, weekly, and monthly performance tracking and analytics.

D. Travel & Reimbursement Automation
   - Automated travel distance logging based on GPS and odometer data.
   - Dynamic travel rates per kilometer.
   - Daily travel summaries and automated allowance calculations with approval workflows.

E. Comprehensive HRMS & Payroll
   - Leave Management: Leave type allocations (Sick, Casual, Earned), application workflows, and loss of pay (LOP) tracking.
   - Salary Structures: Basic, HRA, TA/DA, Allowances, PF, and Tax deductions.
   - Payroll Generation: Automated monthly payroll history generation factoring in LOP and allowances.
   - Professional, print-ready payslip generation with embedded profile bank details.

F. Reporting & Analytics
   - Advanced filtering for daily and monthly administrative reports.
   - Dynamic multi-format data export (CSV/PDF) for individuals and teams.
   - Administrative dashboards for overall operational insights.

4. TECHNICAL STACK HIGHLIGHTS
---------------------------------------------------------
- Frontend (Client-Side): 
  - HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript.
  - Responsive, mobile-first design with a modern, high-end UI (glassmorphism, micro-animations, custom color palettes).
  - Dynamic, asynchronous interactions, icon-based controls, and modal-driven workflows.
- Backend (Server-Side):
  - PHP (Custom MVC Architecture - Models/Views/Controllers).
  - Secure RESTful APIs with JavaScript Fetch integration.
  - Custom session and token-based authentication with auto-logout features.
- Database:
  - MySQL relational database with optimized indexing and foreign key constraints.
  - Robust historical data preservation (e.g., payroll history, task audit trails).
- Third-Party Integrations & Services:
  - HERE Maps API for Geocoding and Reverse Geocoding (with local DB caching for optimization).
  - Dynamic PDF Generation libraries for reports, ID cards, and payslips.
  - Native CSV export handlers.
